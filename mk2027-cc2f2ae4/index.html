The plan at a glance

Thirty-two days of leave buy six weeks in Europe, Tue 27 Apr to Mon 7 Jun 2027. Nothing is fixed yet, so the dates follow the local calendar: each leg sits between the school holidays, long weekends and festivals that would fill it (see Fixed dates and assumptions). The route runs north to south and back: Annecy, Turin, the Riviera, the Eolie, Malta, Puglia and Crete as a couple, then Milan, Venice and Rome with Luca. It's also your first long trip together, so it has time apart built in, flexible bookings for the second half, and an easy way home for Kelly after Crete.

  • Annecy, 27 Apr – 1 May. Land in Geneva on Tuesday for four nights, after the local school holidays and before Ascension; Giulia comes down from Paris when it suits her.

  • Turin and Camogli, 1 – 4 May. A night in Turin, then two in Camogli, gone before Ascension and the fish festival on 8 – 9 May.

  • Eolie, 4 – 9 May. Fly Genoa to Catania on Tuesday; Salina for three nights, then Stromboli for two.

  • Malta and Gozo, 9 – 14 May. Fly Catania to Malta on Sunday afternoon; a night in Valletta, then four on Gozo, where the family may have its roots.

  • Puglia, 14 – 20 May. Fly Malta to Bari on Friday; a masseria in the Valle d'Itria for the weekend, with Lecce as a day out, then Matera.

  • Crete, 20 – 28 May. Via Athens to Chania; Apokoronas villages, then Loutro on the car-free south coast. Kelly can fly home from here if she'd rather.

  • Italy with Luca, 28 May – 7 Jun. Milan, Brianza, Monza, Como and Maranello over the Republic Day bridge, when the Milanese leave town; Venice after it; Rome last, after the Giro finale. Caroline collects Luca in Rome on Sun 6 Jun, and you fly home on Mon 7, landing Tue 8 Jun.

Giulia can take leave, so her visit to Annecy isn't tied to a holiday weekend; Annecy is about 3h45 from Paris by TGV.

Luca's dates are a proposal: he'd miss part of NSW Term 2, which is Caroline's call. Vittoria is still at school, so she's with you in the evenings from 28 May, on the weekend of 29 – 30 May and on Republic Day (2 June). Giulia could come to Milan that weekend to see Luca.

Where the hidden savings are

  • Skip Reno. Pick up a car at San Francisco airport, drive to Tahoe and back (about 3.5 h each way) and return it there before the flight to Venice: no extra flight and no one-way fee.

  • Turkish Airlines' round-the-world fare for the late-summer trip. It goes from Australia via North America and Istanbul to Europe, and back via Istanbul and Asia, with free stopovers: the late-summer shape (Sydney → San Francisco, stop, → Istanbul → Venice; Venice → Istanbul → Singapore → Sydney). Australian Frequent Flyer's sample of that shape cost 2,105 in economy and 7,862 in business in 2024. It's sold through agents only and prices may have moved, so get a quote before buying the late-summer legs.

  • Mix airlines in business: TAP via Lisbon, San Francisco → Venice, is 3,606 against 5,306 on United.

  • Book the bag with the low-cost hops (Ryanair Genoa → Catania, Catania → Malta, Malta → Bari and Bari → Athens); it costs more at the airport.

  • Leave buffers on separate tickets. Bags don't transfer between them, so allow at least three hours, or a night, on self-made connections.

Checked and not worth it

  • A return starting in Italy to cover both the June flight home and the flight back out: about 3,000, more than two one-ways.

  • One round-the-world ticket for both trips: they now run in opposite directions.

  • Hidden-city tricks: they put the later legs and checked bags at risk.

When to book

  • Now: the spring long-haul as one open-jaw return on one airline (Sydney → Geneva, Rome → Sydney) for both of you, which usually prices well below two one-ways; changeable fares suit a first long trip together. Hold the late-summer flights until after spring.

  • Two to four months out: Ryanair, Aegean, the Liberty Lines hydrofoils once the spring timetable is out around February, and the Puglia and Crete hire cars.

  • Also early: Annecy, Turin, Camogli, Salina, Stromboli, Valletta, Gozo, the Puglia masseria, Matera and Loutro. Book the second half, from Crete on, with free cancellation.
