The plan at a glance

Twenty days of leave buy four weeks in Europe, Mon 10 May to Sat 5 Jun 2027, flying out on Sunday. The trip is planned backwards from the end: ten days with Luca, from Rome up to Milan, before you fly home, with Kelly leaving for Maine on 27 May. Before that come four days in Amsterdam, Giulia's long weekend in Annecy, then the Riviera and Puglia, warm but before the summer crowds. The other 10 days of spring long service leave stay in the bank.

  • Amsterdam, 10 – 15 May. Land at Schiphol on Monday for four full days in Amsterdam.

  • Annecy, 15 – 18 May. Fly in from Amsterdam on Saturday for three nights over the Whitsun weekend; Giulia comes down from Paris by train. Then the train under the Alps to the Riviera.

  • Liguria, 18 – 20 May. Two midweek nights in Camogli for San Fruttuoso and Portofino.

  • Puglia, 20 – 25 May. Four full days: a masseria in the Valle d'Itria over the weekend, with Lecce as a day out, then Matera (5 nights).

  • Italy with Luca and Vittoria, 25 May – 4 Jun. Three days in Rome, with Kelly until she flies to Maine on 27 May, then Venice with Vittoria for the weekend, and Milan, Monza and Ferrari. Caroline collects Luca on Fri 4 Jun for the UK, and you fly home, landing Sat 5 Jun.

Giulia joins you in Annecy for the Whitsun long weekend (Whit Monday, 17 May, is a French public holiday), about 3h45 from Paris by TGV.

Luca comes last, from Tue 25 May, so he misses part of NSW Term 2; that's Caroline's call. Vittoria is still at school, so she joins you in Venice for the weekend of 29–30 May, then in the evenings and on 2 June, Italy's Republic Day. Luca doesn't overlap with Giulia unless she comes to Milan for a weekend.

Where the hidden savings are

  • Same fare, an extra day. The early Qatar connection from Athens lands the next day, not two days later: leave Chania on Thu 16 Sep instead of Wed 15 and you still land on Fri 17 Sep at the same fare, a free extra night in Crete with no extra leave.

  • Skip Reno. Pick up a car at San Francisco airport, drive to Tahoe and back (about 3.5 h each way) and return it there before the flight to Venice: no extra flight and no one-way fee.

  • Turkish Airlines' round-the-world fare for the late-summer trip. It goes from Australia via North America and Istanbul to Europe, and back from Europe via Istanbul and Asia, with an open jaw in Europe and free stopovers: exactly the late-summer shape (Sydney → San Francisco, stop, → Istanbul → Venice; Athens → Istanbul → Singapore → Sydney). Australian Frequent Flyer's sample of the same shape cost 2,105 in economy and 7,862 in business in 2024, against about 4,100 and 18,400 here, and it undercuts the Star Alliance fare (about 11,000 – 16,000 business). It's sold through agents only and prices may have moved since 2024, so get a quote before buying the late-summer legs; you'd add a Chania → Athens hop.

  • Mix airlines in business: TAP via Lisbon, San Francisco → Venice, is 3,606 against 5,306 on United.

  • Book the bag with the low-cost hops (Wizz Venice → Catania, Ryanair Genoa → Bari); it costs more at the airport.

  • Leave buffers on separate tickets. Bags don't transfer between them, so allow at least three hours, or a night, on self-made connections.

Checked and not worth it

  • A return starting in Italy to cover both the June flight home and the flight back out: about 3,000, more than two one-ways.

  • One round-the-world ticket for both trips: they now run in opposite directions.

  • Hidden-city tricks: they put the later legs and checked bags at risk.

When to book

  • Now: the spring long-haul as one open-jaw return on one airline (Sydney → Amsterdam, Milan → Sydney), which usually prices well below two one-ways, and Kelly's Rome → Maine. Get the Turkish quote before booking Sydney → San Francisco, San Francisco → Venice and Chania → Sydney.

  • Two to four months out: Ryanair, Wizz and Aegean.

  • Also early: Amsterdam, Annecy for the Whitsun weekend, Rome and the nights in Venice, and Camogli and the Puglia masseria for late May.
